## New findings worth knowing

- **Sign rule for `poke`:** a sign means "change by" (`+20`, `-5`); no sign sets the value; a negative value is `to -50`. zsh reads a word that starts with `=` as a command, so `=-50` needs quotes.
- Experience: the game's own table (in memory, data segment `+0x1D74`) is 0, 20, 60, 120, 200, 300, 450, 600, 1000. An ordinary unit tops out at Elite (120); with the **Warlord** retort the game shows it one level higher, Ultra Elite. A hero tops out at Demi-God (1000, level 8).
- A summoned creature's unit has no weapon grade and does not level; the **low two bits of `mutations` (`+23`)** are the weapon grade of an ordinary unit.
- Unpaid mana at End Turn: the game disbands the wizard's **summoned creatures** and dissipates **every unit enchantment**.
- The game draws stacks from a picture it builds earlier: a hand edit to a unit (`pew`, a move) shows as a ghost until the map view is nudged.
- **A tool mistake to remember:** `diff --ignore` with a snapshot from another moment hides real changes as noise (it made the real Time Stop look like a total freeze). Now refused.
